Miss against the detector

Where the placed mannequin’s joints sit against where the detector says yours are. Read the percentage, not the pixels: ten pixels is nothing up close and a great deal across the room.

The detector runs at a fraction of the redraw rate on purpose, the way it would on a phone. Between detections the pose is carried forward by its own velocity, with the filter’s lag taken back out — turn prediction off and the figure visibly drops behind you. Mirroring flips the picture and the overlay together for comfort; the detector always sees the true frame, so left stays left.
